Privacy Policy

Al Shariq Enterprises

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Al Shariq Enterprises collects, uses, stores, and protects information when customers communicate with us through WhatsApp and related business messaging services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name and contact details, including your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Messages, enquiries, and information you send to us through WhatsApp.
  • Information related to travel enquiries, bookings, packages, payments, or customer support.
  • Documents or media that you voluntarily send to us for providing requested services.
  • Technical message information made available by the WhatsApp Business Platform, such as message identifiers and delivery status.

2. How We Use Information

  • Responding to customer enquiries and providing customer support.
  • Providing information about Hajj, Umrah, ticketing, travel, hotels, transport, and related services.
  • Managing leads, bookings, customer records, and service requests.
  • Processing and verifying customer-provided payment information where applicable.
  • Improving our customer service and internal business processes.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, duplicate requests, and unauthorized activity.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

Our messaging integration may use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta. Messages and related technical data may therefore be processed by Meta in accordance with Meta's and WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Automated Assistance

We may use automated systems, including our internal JARVIS business assistant, to help classify enquiries, draft replies, maintain conversation context, and assist with customer service. Important business actions, including payment verification, may require human review or approval.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or work with service providers that support our business operations.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, business records, legal or regulatory requirements, dispute resolution, and legitimate business purposes.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information that we hold, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

To request deletion of information associated with your WhatsApp conversations or our business messaging systems, contact us using the email address below and include enough information for us to identify the relevant record.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed specifically to children. Where information relating to a minor is required for travel or booking purposes, it should be provided by a parent, guardian, or authorized adult.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
